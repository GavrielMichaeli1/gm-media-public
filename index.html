Welcome to G.M Media.

A private, non-commercial personal media library and streaming application for organizing and watching movies, TV series, music, podcasts and filmed stage musicals through a unified desktop and browser experience.

🎬

Discover

Organize locally held movies and series with posters, descriptions, seasons and cast information.

▶️

Watch

Playback with subtitles, audio options, profiles, watch history and resume support.

✨

Artwork

Optional artwork and metadata integrations bring cinematic visual presentation.

Fanart.tv artwork integration

G.M Media may retrieve and locally cache posters, backdrops, and logos for identified titles through Fanart.tv, subject to its terms. Intended solely for personal, non-commercial use.
